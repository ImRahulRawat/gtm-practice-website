Insurance Knowledge

Everything You Need to Know About Insurance

Understand different types of insurance, explore essential policy features and learn how to make informed coverage decisions.

Motor Insurance

Motor insurance helps protect vehicle owners against covered financial losses arising from accidents, damage and third-party liabilities.

Types of Motor Insurance
  • Third-Party Insurance: Covers eligible third-party liabilities as specified under the policy.
  • Own-Damage Insurance: Covers specified damage to your own vehicle, subject to policy terms.
  • Comprehensive Insurance: Combines third-party liability cover with own-damage cover, subject to the policy wording.
Useful tip: Review deductibles, exclusions, add-ons and the insured declared value (IDV) before purchasing a policy.

Health Insurance

Health insurance can help manage eligible medical expenses and reduce the financial burden of covered hospitalisation and treatment.

Key Features to Understand
  • Sum Insured: The coverage limit available under the policy, subject to its conditions.
  • Waiting Period: A specified period before certain benefits become available.
  • Cashless Treatment: Eligible treatment at a network hospital under the insurer's applicable process.
  • Pre-existing Conditions: Check applicable waiting periods, disclosures and coverage conditions.
Useful tip: Compare hospital networks, room-rent limits, co-payments and exclusions.

Term Insurance

Term life insurance provides financial protection to beneficiaries if the insured person dies during the covered policy term, subject to policy conditions.

Important Considerations
  • Life Cover: Choose coverage after considering family expenses, liabilities and future financial needs.
  • Policy Term: Consider how long your dependants may need financial protection.
  • Premium: Understand the premium amount, payment frequency and applicable conditions.
  • Disclosure: Provide accurate information about health, occupation and lifestyle.
Useful tip: Review exclusions, claim conditions and beneficiary details carefully.
